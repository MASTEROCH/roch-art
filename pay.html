02Способ оплаты

Перед оплатой я пришлю счёт. В нём есть его номер и адрес получателя, если банк их спросит. Номер счёта можно добавить в назначение платежа.

Choose what you are paying for and a convenient method. Tap any line of the details to copy it. After the transfer, send a confirmation on Telegram, and I will let you know when the money arrives.

02Payment method

I send an invoice before you pay. It has its number and the beneficiary address, in case your bank asks for them. You can add the invoice number to the payment reference.

If your bank asks who pays the fees, choose OUR, so the full amount arrives.

An international transfer usually takes one to three business days.

USDT · TRON (TRC-20)

Scan the code with your wallet or copy the address below.

Send only USDT and only on the TRON network (TRC-20). A transfer on another network will not arrive and cannot be returned.

Before sending, check the start and the end of the address: TJ3p … 9VvN.

If you withdraw from an exchange, make sure the amount to be received is the full sum: the exchange may deduct its fee from the transfer.

03Send a confirmation

Send a screenshot of the transfer or the transaction hash on Telegram. The message is ready: it has the amount, the method and your gift code if you opened the bonus on the site.
